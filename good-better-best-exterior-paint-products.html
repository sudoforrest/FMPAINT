Quick Answer: Which Exterior Paint Should You Choose?

For most Oklahoma homeowners, Sherwin-Williams SuperPaint is a strong all-around exterior paint and is typically the minimum product we recommend. A-100 can work for budget-friendly projects, Duration is better for long-term durability, and Emerald Rain Refresh is ideal for homes that get dirty and need a cleaner-looking finish between washes.

Good: A-100 Budget-friendly refreshes, rentals, and short-term ownership.
Better: SuperPaint Best all-around value for most full exterior repaints.
Premium Durability: Duration Best when you want maximum life and are not changing colors soon.
Premium Self-Cleaning: Emerald Rain Refresh Best for homes prone to dirt, pollen, algae, and water streaking.

When homeowners start comparing exterior paint products, one of the most common questions is simple: what is actually worth paying for?

The truth is that not every home needs the most expensive paint available. But choosing the cheapest product possible can cost more long term if the home needs to be repainted sooner.

At F&M Paint, we typically recommend Sherwin-Williams products because we have had good real-world experience with them in Oklahoma weather. We also generally use SuperPaint as our minimum recommended exterior product because we believe it provides a better balance of durability, appearance, and long-term value than entry-level paints.

Tier 1: Good

Sherwin-Williams A-100: Budget-Friendly Exterior Paint

A-100 is generally considered an entry-level exterior paint. That does not mean it is bad, but it is designed more for affordability than long-term premium performance.

For some projects, that may make perfect sense.

Good fit for:

  • Rental properties
  • Investment homes
  • Temporary refreshes
  • Homes being sold soon
  • Lower-budget repaint projects

Advantages

  • Lower material cost
  • Fresh color on the home
  • Affordable for larger budget-sensitive projects
  • Decent initial appearance when properly applied

Limitations

Compared to higher-tier products, A-100 generally has lower fade resistance, lower flexibility, less durability, and a shorter expected lifespan in harsh Oklahoma conditions. For homeowners planning to stay in the home long term, we usually recommend stepping up to at least SuperPaint.

Tier 2: Better

Sherwin-Williams SuperPaint: Our Standard Recommended Exterior Product

SuperPaint is typically the minimum exterior product we recommend at F&M Paint.

Why? Because most of the cost of exterior painting is labor, prep work, masking, washing, scraping, caulking, setup, and application quality. The paint matters, but it is only one part of the total investment.

Upgrading from an entry-level coating to a better exterior system is often a relatively small percentage of the overall project cost, but it can make a noticeable difference in durability and long-term appearance.

SuperPaint is a great all-around option for:

  • Most Oklahoma homes
  • Homeowners wanting solid long-term value
  • Siding and trim repaints
  • Brick accents
  • Full exterior transformations

Advantages

  • Stronger durability than entry-level paints
  • Better flexibility during Oklahoma temperature swings
  • Improved fade resistance
  • Good value-to-performance ratio
  • Reliable all-around exterior performance

For many homes, SuperPaint is the sweet spot.

Tier 3: Premium Durability

Sherwin-Williams Duration: Best for Long-Term Exterior Protection

Duration is typically the product we recommend when homeowners want the maximum practical lifespan out of their repaint and are not planning to change colors again anytime soon.

This can be especially useful in Oklahoma, where sun exposure, wind, storms, and temperature swings can be hard on exterior surfaces.

Good fit for:

  • Long-term homeowners
  • High sun exposure homes
  • Weather-exposed elevations
  • Homeowners wanting longer repaint cycles
  • Homes where durability is more important than lowest upfront cost

Advantages

  • Increased durability
  • Stronger moisture resistance
  • Longer-lasting finish
  • Excellent flexibility
  • Better protection in harsh weather conditions

Duration often makes the most sense when homeowners plan to keep the same exterior color scheme for many years. Since the coating system is designed for longevity, it may not provide as much value if the homeowner expects to repaint again soon for style reasons.

Tier 4: Premium Self-Cleaning

Sherwin-Williams Emerald Rain Refresh: Best for Homes That Get Dirty

Emerald Rain Refresh is one of the most unique exterior products because it is designed to help the home stay cleaner over time.

Instead of dirt and debris clinging as easily to the surface, rainwater is designed to help rinse contaminants away more effectively.

Great fit for:

  • Homes near trees
  • Shaded homes
  • Areas prone to pollen
  • Homes that collect dust or algae
  • White or light-colored exteriors
  • Homeowners wanting a lower-maintenance appearance

Advantages

  • Self-cleaning technology
  • Excellent color retention
  • Premium overall appearance
  • Strong durability
  • Reduced dirt buildup over time
  • Cleaner appearance between washes

For homeowners choosing lighter colors, especially whites or soft neutrals, Rain Refresh can help the home maintain a cleaner look longer without requiring as much manual washing.

Which Sherwin-Williams Exterior Paint Is Best?

There is not one perfect product for every situation. The best choice depends on your budget, how long you plan to stay in the home, your desired appearance, your maintenance expectations, weather exposure, and your long-term goals.

Paint Quality Matters, But Prep Matters More

A quality exterior paint job is about much more than the product on the can. Prep work, caulking, cleaning, primer selection, masking, and application quality all play a major role in how long the finish lasts.

A premium paint applied over a poorly prepared surface can still fail early. A good exterior system should include the right product and the right process.

Final Thoughts

At F&M Paint, we work with homeowners to help choose the right exterior system for their goals, budget, and home style instead of pushing a one-size-fits-all approach.

For most homes, SuperPaint is the minimum product we recommend because it gives a strong balance of value and durability. Duration is a great upgrade for long-term performance, and Emerald Rain Refresh is an excellent choice for homeowners who want the exterior to stay cleaner with less maintenance.

If you are considering repainting your exterior and want help deciding which paint system makes the most sense for your home, we would be happy to help.

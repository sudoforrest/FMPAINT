Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

A few quick answers to help you understand how we work before we ever step on site.

What exterior surfaces do you paint?

We work with a wide range of exterior surfaces, including wood, siding, trim, stucco, brick, and metal. During the initial walkthrough, we evaluate each surface and build the right approach to ensure the finished result looks clean and lasts.

Do you help with color selection?

Yes. We offer guidance and can connect you with color consultants to help you feel confident in your choices. Our goal is to make sure the final look fits your home and your vision before any work begins.

What paint do you use?

We use premium Sherwin-Williams products and select the right system based on your home’s materials and condition. We typically offer good, better, and best options so you can choose the level of durability and long-term performance that fits your goals.

Do you handle repairs before painting?

Yes, when needed. During the walkthrough, we identify any areas that may require repair and discuss the best way to address them before painting begins so the final result looks complete.

How long does a typical exterior project take?

Most exterior projects take 2–5 days depending on the size and complexity. We prioritize doing the work correctly and maintaining a clean, organized job site each day.

Do you require a deposit?

For most projects, we require a 50% deposit to secure your spot on the schedule, with the remaining balance due upon completion. Larger projects may follow a custom payment structure depending on scope.

Do you offer gutters, siding, windows, or doors?

Yes. We can coordinate gutters, siding, windows, and doors through trusted partners so your entire exterior project comes together the right way.

Can I call instead of filling out the form?

Absolutely. You can call us directly at (405) 697-7828 if you prefer to talk through your project instead of submitting a form.

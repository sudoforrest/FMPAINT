Our Process

How We Paint Your Home

A great exterior finish starts with the prep. Here’s what you can expect when you hire F&M Paint.

1

Pressure Washing

We remove dirt, chalking, and mildew so the new paint can properly adhere.

2

Scrape & Sand Prep

Loose paint is removed and surfaces are smoothed to create a solid base for the new coating.

3

Caulking & Small Repairs

We seal gaps and joints to prevent moisture intrusion and deliver crisp, clean lines.

4

Masking & Protection

Windows, brick, fixtures, and landscaping are carefully protected before we start application.

5

Professional Application

We apply high-quality coatings using professional tools for an even, durable finish.

6

Final Walkthrough

We do a final inspection and touch-ups so you’re proud of the finished result.
